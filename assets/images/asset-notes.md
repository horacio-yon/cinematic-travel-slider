# Asset provenance and generation prompts

All five PNG assets were created for this project using the built-in ImageGen tool. These are AI-generated photographic compositions, not documentary photographs of the named places. Expedition statistics and routes are illustrative. No external service is requested at runtime.

The source reference images remain untouched in ../referencias (relative to the project directory). They guided layout, proportions, colors and interaction; they are not used as page backgrounds.

## Shared landscape composition prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: cinematic photographic travel website background, landscape 16:9. A perfectly straight narrow wet black asphalt road, no painted markings, starts at bottom edges x=15% and 85%, converges to exact center x=50%, y=55%. Flat wilderness either side. Camera eye height, no people, cars or buildings. Bottom 45% road and detailed foreground, top 55% landscape and sky. Premium realistic expedition photography, real textured asphalt, fine natural terrain, no geometric illustration, no low-poly, no UI, no text.

## patagonia.png

A straight dark wet road through flat tawny Patagonian steppe to a central vanishing point. Vast detailed snow-covered glaciated mountains, slate-grey dramatic storm clouds, luminous band of white mist on the horizon. Cold white, soft grey, glacier blue, subtle warm dry grass. Premium real expedition photography, realistic asphalt texture and subtle reflections. Human eye-height camera. No painted markings, people, cars, text, interface or borders.

## arctic.png

Shared landscape composition. Northern Norway at polar blue hour. Almost flat treeless cold steppe, tiny distant dark snowy peaks. Enormous deep navy and violet night sky with fine subtle stars, soft cyan aurora drifting overhead, bright thin coral-red dawn glow on the horizon. Rich elegant deep blue, purple, coral light reflecting faintly in wet road, tan frost-covered tundra. Photographically believable, atmospheric, dramatic but not oversaturated.

## tundra.png

Shared landscape composition. Expansive Icelandic glacial highlands, open minimal flat frost-covered tundra, remote pale glacier ridge at horizon. Brilliant ice-cyan sky, large beautiful white clouds with subtle grey depth, pale luminous horizon. Snow patches and muted pale brown arctic grasses either side of asphalt. Clean crisp ice blue, cyan and white with atmospheric sunlit haze. Authentic landscape photograph with soft pale daylight and fine photographic texture.

## vulcan.png

Shared landscape composition. Remote volcanic highlands at pastel sunset, low black lava fields either side of road, distant volcanic cones and soft smoke. Sky grades from dusty lavender and pink to pale apricot-orange glow at horizon. A dark volcano rises beside the road without obstructing the vanishing point. Wet road reflects warm horizon subtly. Moody real landscape photograph, warm cinematic color, rich natural basalt textures. No fantasy molten lava.

## traveler.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. A transparent PNG cutout for a premium travel website. One realistic full-body adult traveler seen exactly from behind, centered, both boots visible, standing still looking toward a distant landscape. Natural relaxed neutral pose, arms hanging loosely, feet slightly separated, no walking stride. Deep navy technical hiking hooded jacket with hood down, dark slim trekking trousers, worn tan leather hiking boots, compact muted dark teal technical adventure backpack with realistic padded shoulder straps and small buckles. Short ash-brown hair. Lean natural proportions, real fabric texture, wrinkles and stitching. Soft overcast daylight from above and behind, slightly brighter shoulder edges. Photographic realism, not illustration, vector or 3D. Genuine transparent background, no scenery, ground or text. Chest-height camera, full-body back view, minimal unused margins. No studio glow or outlines.
